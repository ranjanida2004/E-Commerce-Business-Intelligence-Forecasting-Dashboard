# 📊 E-Commerce Business Intelligence & Forecasting Dashboard

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project is a comprehensive end-to-end Power BI dashboard solution built to analyze e-commerce business performance across sales, customers, forecasting, and operations.

The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into actionable business insights using:

- Data Modeling
- Advanced DAX Calculations
- Forecasting Analytics
- Operational KPIs
- Interactive Visualizations

---

## 🧠 Business Objectives

- Monitor revenue and overall sales performance  
- Analyze customer behavior and segmentation  
- Track business growth trends  
- Forecast future revenue and profit  
- Identify operational inefficiencies and return impact  

---

## 📑 Dashboard Structure (5 Pages)

---

### 🏆 1️⃣ Executive Overview

High-level KPIs for management decision-making:

- Total Revenue  
- Total Orders  
- Total Customers  
- Average Order Value  
- Monthly Growth %  
- YOY Growth %  

📌 Provides a quick snapshot of overall business health.

---

### 📈 2️⃣ Sales Performance Analytics

Deep dive into sales performance:

- Revenue by Category  
- Revenue by Region  
- Monthly Revenue Trend  
- Top Performing Products  
- Growth Analysis  

🔎 Includes dynamic slicers for:
- Year  
- Region  
- Category  

---

### 👥 3️⃣ Customer Analytics (Advanced)

Customer behavior analysis:

- Sales by Age Group  
- Gender Distribution  
- Region-wise Customer Sales  
- Repeat Purchase Rate  
- Top 20 High-Value Customers  

#### 🔥 Advanced: RFM Segmentation

Customers classified into:

- High Value  
- Loyal  
- At Risk  

🎯 Helps identify retention strategies and revenue opportunities.

---

### 📊 4️⃣ Trend & Forecasting Analytics

Growth tracking and predictive analysis:

- Revenue Forecast (95% Confidence Interval)  
- Moving Average (3-Month)  
- YOY Growth %  
- Monthly Growth %  
- Running Total Revenue  
- Profit Trend Analysis  

📌 Forecasting supports strategic planning decisions.

---

### 🚚 5️⃣ Operations & Returns Analytics

Operational efficiency monitoring:

- Return Rate by Category  
- Delivery Time Trend  
- Payment Failure %  
- Refund Impact  
- Shipping & Operational Cost Analysis  

⚠ Conditional formatting highlights high-risk categories.
